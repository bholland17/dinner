Connect your apps
  • Your calendarImport an .ics export and every evening you're booked (5 to 10 PM) gets marked can't. All-day events become if I must. Google Calendar: Settings, Import & export, Export. Apple Calendar on a Mac: File, Export. Outlook: File, Save Calendar.
  • Group textShare and Nudge open your phone's share sheet, so the link goes to iMessage, WhatsApp, GroupMe or Slack.
  • CalendarsOnce a night is locked, everyone gets one-tap buttons for Google Calendar, Outlook, and Apple Calendar.
  • ReservationsLocked dinners link to OpenTable for a table for four, plus Google Maps for the spot.
